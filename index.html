Trackypady

Set up your Mac.

Trackypady turns your iPhone into a gesture controller for Codex on your Mac. The developer release will be available on GitHub.

View Mac releases
  1. Download the Trackypady Bridge DMG on your Mac and open Bridge.
  2. Scan the QR with your iPhone Camera, then tap the link.
  3. If you install the iPhone app after scanning, open it and scan the same QR again.
